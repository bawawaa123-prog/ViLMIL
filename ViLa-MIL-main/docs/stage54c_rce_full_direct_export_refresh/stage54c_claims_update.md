# Stage54C Claims Update

## Claims Now Supported

- Full and w/o CSG evidence are both exported through the Step54 evidence pipeline.
- Full vs w/o CSG evidence-level comparisons can be used as matched interpretability illustrations with explicit variant provenance.
- CSG may affect concept evidence ranking and confidence behavior in selected cases.
- Region-concept evidence visualizations provide interpretable clues for how the RCE main model and the `wo_csg` variant behave on matched slides.

## Claims Still Not Supported

- CSG significantly or substantially improves ACC.
- Pathologist-level localization.
- Heatmaps or concept evidence are equivalent to expert annotations.
- Statistically significant localization improvement.
- Evidence visualization is a strict localization benchmark.
- Full vs w/o CSG evidence figures are proof of training-time statistical significance.

## Practical Paper Boundary

- The refreshed Step54B figures can now be cited as same-pipeline direct-export interpretability figures.
- They should still be framed as qualitative or semi-structured evidence illustrations, not as a substitute for a formal localization study.
